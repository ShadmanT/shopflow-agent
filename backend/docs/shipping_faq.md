# ShopFlow Shipping FAQ

## Standard shipping times
- Standard (free over $35): 5-7 business days
- Expedited ($9.99): 2-3 business days
- Overnight ($24.99): next business day if ordered before 1pm EST

## Order processing
Orders placed before 2pm EST on business days ship the same day. Orders placed after 2pm or on weekends ship the next business day.

## Tracking
Tracking numbers are emailed when the order ships, usually within 1 business day of placing the order. Allow 24 hours for tracking to update after receiving the email.

## Lost or stolen packages
If tracking shows delivered but you haven't received your package, wait 24 hours as carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early. If still missing after 24 hours, contact support and we will investigate with the carrier and reship or refund.

## International shipping
ShopFlow ships to Canada, UK, and Australia. International orders take 10-14 business days. Customers are responsible for customs duties and import taxes.

## Address changes
Address changes can be made within 1 hour of placing an order. After that, we cannot guarantee changes can be applied before the order ships.
