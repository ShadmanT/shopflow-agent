# ShopFlow Returns Policy

## Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

## How to return
Contact support to initiate a return. We will email a prepaid shipping label within 24 hours. Once we receive the item, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days.

## Non-returnable items
The following cannot be returned:
- Items marked as final sale
- Opened personal care products
- Digital downloads

## Damaged or defective items
If your item arrived damaged or defective, we will replace it or issue a full refund with no return required. Contact support within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the damage.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free for damaged, defective, or incorrect items. For all other returns, a $6.99 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.
