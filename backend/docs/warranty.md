# ShopFlow Warranty Policy

## Standard warranty
All ShopFlow products come with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use.

## What is covered
- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failures under normal use
- Premature wear not caused by misuse

## What is not covered
- Accidental damage (drops, spills, cracks)
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from misuse or modification
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function

## How to claim warranty
Contact support with your order number and a description of the issue. For electronics, we may request photos or a short video demonstrating the defect. Approved claims are replaced or refunded within 5 business days.

## Extended warranty
ShopFlow Plus members receive an extended 2-year warranty on all electronics purchases automatically.

## Electronics-specific warranty
Batteries are covered for 6 months only, as battery degradation over time is considered normal wear. Accessories such as cables and charging adapters are covered for 90 days.
